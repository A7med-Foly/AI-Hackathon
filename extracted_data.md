# Type 2 diabetes in adults: management (NG28)

## 1.4 Starting treatment

### 1.4.1 Lifestyle modifications
Advise lifestyle changes including dietary advice, weight management, and physical activity structured for adults with type 2 diabetes.

### 1.4.2 Pharmacological treatment
Offer standard-release metformin as first-line treatment for adults with type 2 diabetes. If metformin is contraindicated or not tolerated, consider an SGLT2 inhibitor or DPP-4 inhibitor.

### 1.4.3 Monitoring after initiation
Measure HbA1c levels every 3 to 6 months until stable on unchanging therapy, then every 6 months thereafter.
